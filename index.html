About

DJ ZOEY is a Shenzhen-based DJ playing house music with a deep, groove-driven pulse — underground house, deep house, minimal and groove house.

Away from the club, her home sessions — "Groove in the Kitchen", balcony nights above the city and sunsets by the sea — have become her signature.

Her sets are warm, hypnotic and built for the long run: rolling basslines, minimal grooves and a dancefloor that keeps moving.
